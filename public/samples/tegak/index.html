06CASE STUDY

18,400 m² ofproduction floor,in 14 months.

PROJECTIndustrial Manufacturing Facility
LOCATIONKarawang, West Java
SCOPEDesign & Build
AREA18,400 m²
DURATION14 Months
01 — CHALLENGE

A fixed production start date, soft reclaimed ground, and a 24 m clear-span requirement for overhead cranes — with design still incomplete at award.

02 — OUR APPROACH

Bored-pile foundations designed in parallel with the steel package; prefabricated portal frames erected in two fronts while MEP rough-in followed bay by bay.

03 — RESULT

Handed over 9 days ahead of the contract date with zero lost-time incidents across 610,000 working hours.
